    ### [A Discrete-Choice-Driven Update Frequency Framework for Cycle-Wise Offset Adaptation](offset-adaptation.md)

    *Ongoing · M.Tech research, IIT Bombay*

    How often should a coordinated corridor re-compute its offsets? A discrete choice formulation that decides, cycle by cycle, whether an update is worth making on a vehicle-actuated corridor.

    `Discrete choice modelling` · `Signal coordination` · `PTV VISSIM`

    Diagnosing and redesigning a problematic roadway segment inside the IIT Bombay campus, from field data collection through to a proposed geometric and control treatment.
