# Traffic Design & Management of a Problematic Roadway Segment, IIT Bombay Campus

![Cover image](../assets/images/iitb-campus-traffic-cover.png)

!!! note "Status"
    Ongoing project at IIT Bombay.

## Overview

The IIT Bombay campus carries an unusual traffic mix — buses, cars, two-wheelers, cyclists and a very large volume of pedestrians — on a road network that was never designed for the demand it now sees. Certain segments have become recurring conflict points, with pedestrian crossings, parking manoeuvres and through movements all competing for the same space.

This project takes one such problematic segment, diagnoses why it fails, and proposes a design and management treatment.

## Objectives

- Quantify the operational and safety problems on the selected segment through field data
- Identify the specific geometric and behavioural causes rather than the visible symptoms
- Propose a treatment covering geometry, traffic control and management measures
- Test the proposed treatment against the existing condition in simulation

## Methodology

**Field data collection.** Classified volume counts, pedestrian counts, speed measurement and conflict observation across representative peak and off-peak periods.

**Diagnosis.** Analysis of capacity, level of service, and the location and type of recurring traffic conflicts, mapped onto the segment geometry.

**Design.** Development of a treatment — cross-section reallocation, pedestrian crossing provision, parking regulation and signage/marking — responding to the diagnosed causes.

**Evaluation.** Micro-simulation of existing and proposed conditions in **PTV VISSIM**, compared on delay, speed and conflict measures.

## Tools & Methods

- **PTV VISSIM** — micro-simulation of existing and proposed conditions
- **QGIS** — mapping of the segment, land use and pedestrian desire lines
- **AutoCAD Civil 3D** — geometric design drawings
- **R** — analysis of survey data
